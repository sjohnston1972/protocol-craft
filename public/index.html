Protocol Craft

Infinite Craft, for the network stack.

Start with eight raw elements — copper, glass, a clock, a bare number — and combine them two at a time to craft your way up the OSI model. From a single electron to Ethernet, to IP and BGP, all the way to a padlock in the address bar. Around 470 real protocols are hidden in here, Layer 1 to Layer 7. There's no map and no score — just keep combining and see how high up the stack you climb.
